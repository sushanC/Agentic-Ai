### Daily Plan for Sushan

Given your goals and preferences, here's a realistic daily plan focusing on prioritizing important career tasks, including breaks, and incorporating elements of your personal goals.

#### 9:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Morning Routine
- Start with a 30-minute morning routine: 
  - Drink a glass of water (part of the 3 liters goal)
  - Quick 10-minute meditation or deep breathing exercise
  - Plan out your top 3 priority tasks for the day

#### 9:30 AM - 10:30 AM: Update Resume
- Work on updating your resume to prepare for internship applications.

#### 10:30 AM - 10:45 AM: Break
- Short break: Stretch, move around, and refresh your mind.

#### 10:45 AM - 12:15 PM: Apply for Internship
- Dedicate time to applying for internships. Make sure your resume and cover letter are tailored to each position.

#### 12:15 PM - 1:15 PM: Lunch Break
- Take a break for lunch. Try to drink another liter of water during this time.
- Use some time to relax or read something light, like an AI-related blog or news.

#### 1:15 PM - 2:45 PM: Solve LeetCode Problems
- Spend quality time solving LeetCode problems to improve your coding skills, focusing on problems related to AI or system design.

#### 2:45 PM - 3:00 PM: Break
- Take a short break to stretch and refresh your mind.

#### 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM: Learn Node.js
- Dedicate time to learning Node.js, as it's a valuable skill that can complement your AI and Java knowledge.

#### 4:00 PM - 4:30 PM: Break
- Short break: Grab a snack, move around, and maybe drink some more water.

#### 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM: Exercise
- Take 30 minutes to exercise. This could be a brisk walk, some yoga, or any form of physical activity you enjoy.
- Followed by a 30-minute break to shower and refresh.

#### 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM: Dinner Break
- Take time for dinner and try to finish your 3 liters of water goal.
- Relax and unwind during this time.

#### 7:00 PM - 8:00 PM: Build Portfolio Website
- Work on building or updating your portfolio website to showcase your projects and skills.

#### 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM: Review Day and Plan Tomorrow
- Review what you've accomplished today.
- Plan your tasks for tomorrow, ensuring you're moving closer to your goals.
- Clean your workspace to maintain productivity and focus.

#### 9:00 PM - 10:30 PM: Relaxation and Preparation for Bed
- Take some time to relax. Read a book, listen to music, or do something you enjoy that helps you unwind.
- Prepare for bed, aiming to sleep before 11 PM.

This plan prioritizes your career development, includes time for exercise and relaxation, and helps you stay on track with your goals. Remember, flexibility is key, so feel free to adjust the schedule as needed to fit your natural flow and energy levels.
