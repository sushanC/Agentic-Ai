Okay, let's categorize these tasks based on a typical career-focused individual aiming for professional growth and well-being. The priorities assume that securing an internship and building a career are primary current goals.

---

### 1. High Priority

These are tasks that are critical for your immediate professional goals, have deadlines, or directly impact your career progression and skill development for those goals.

*   **Complete Internship:**
    *   **Why:** This is likely an ongoing commitment with deadlines and expectations. Successfully completing an internship is paramount for gaining experience, networking, and potentially securing a full-time offer or strong recommendations. It's a current obligation and a direct path to career advancement.
*   **Find An Internship:**
    *   **Why:** If you don't currently have one (and "Complete Internship" implies you might be seeking or have one ongoing), finding an internship is the gateway to practical experience, skill application, and career building. It's a proactive step towards a defined professional goal.
*   **Learn React:**
    *   **Why:** React is a highly in-demand skill in web development. Learning it directly supports "Build portfolio Website" and significantly increases your chances of "Find An Internship" or future job opportunities, as many companies look for this skill. It's a foundational technical skill for career growth.
*   **Build portfolio Website:**
    *   **Why:** A strong portfolio website is your online resume and showcase of your skills. It's essential for demonstrating your abilities to potential employers when you "Find An Internship" or a job. It directly translates your learned skills (like React) into tangible proof.
*   **Solve Leet Code Problems:**
    *   **Why:** LeetCode problems are a standard part of technical interviews for software development roles and internships. Regularly practicing these problems is crucial for passing interviews and therefore directly supports "Find An Internship" and future job acquisition.

### 2. Medium Priority

These tasks are important for long-term growth, skill enhancement, and resume building, but may not have the same immediate urgency or direct impact as the high-priority items. They often complement or enhance the high-priority tasks.

*   **Contribute in Github OpenSource:**
    *   **Why:** This is an excellent way to gain real-world coding experience, collaborate with others, learn best practices, and build a public track record of your contributions. It significantly enhances your resume and skill set, making you more attractive to employers, but it's generally more flexible and less time-critical than completing an internship or preparing for immediate interviews.

### 3. Low Priority

These tasks are important for personal well-being, relaxation, and leisure. While crucial for preventing burnout and maintaining a healthy balance, they should be scheduled around your higher-priority commitments.

*   **Watch Movies:**
    *   **Why:** Purely for entertainment and relaxation. While necessary for unwinding, it doesn't directly contribute to your professional goals and can be easily deferred if higher-priority tasks require attention.
*   **Play Cricket:**
    *   **Why:** A leisure activity and exercise. Important for physical health and stress relief, but like watching movies, it's not directly tied to your professional development and can be scheduled flexibly.
*   **Take a Nap:**
    *   **Why:** Essential for physical and mental rest, especially when feeling tired. While naps can boost productivity *when needed*, it's generally a reactive action rather than a proactive task. It should be taken as a strategic break to recharge, fitting into your schedule when possible without disrupting higher-priority work. If you're consistently needing naps due to exhaustion, it might indicate a need to re-evaluate your overall schedule and sleep habits.

---

This categorization aims to help you prioritize your efforts to achieve your professional goals effectively while still acknowledging the importance of personal well-being. Good luck!
